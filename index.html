Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Do you buy individual cards or just entire collections?

We buy both! Whether you have a single high-value card or a large collection you've built over years, we're happy to take a look and make an offer.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes! We are open Saturday from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM to accommodate all our collectors.

Do you host local events?

Absolutely. We host and participate in local events like the Whiskey City Card Show. Follow us on social media to stay updated on the next one!

What sports do you cover?

We cover all major sports including Baseball, Football, Basketball, Hockey, and Soccer, as well as TCG like Pokémon and Magic: The Gathering.
